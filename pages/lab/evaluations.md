Feedback in the lab runs on two clocks. Weekly updates ([Communication Rules](/lab/communication/)) keep projects moving; the annual review and quarterly check-ins described here keep *you* moving — toward candidacy, graduation, or the job market. The forms below are deliberately tied to the [DRI Expectations](/lab/expectations/) rubric: the annual review asks you to self-assess against the same DRI categories we use all year, so nothing in it should come as a surprise.

## Annual review

Once a year (typically late spring), we sit down for a structured review. You complete the self-evaluation parts before the meeting; we finalize the coming year's goals together during it. The form opens with last year's goals and their outcomes — the accountability loop matters more than any single rating — and closes with jointly defined goals, each with a deliverable and a target date. Pivoted or dropped goals are expected; research is a stochastic decision process, and what we care about is what the pivot taught us.

* **[Annual Review — PhD Student (fillable PDF)](/assets/pdf/annual-review-phd.pdf)** — milestones and thesis progress, DRI self-assessment, RA and GSI sections, professional development, support needed, and next-year goals.
* **[Annual Review — Postdoc (fillable PDF)](/assets/pdf/annual-review-postdoc.pdf)** — organized around the trajectory toward independence: your own research line, funding and fellowships, mentoring record, and the job market.

Rhythm: I send the form two weeks before the meeting; you return it three days ahead; I complete the advisor sections during or just after; the signed copy is filed in your Drive folder.

## Quarterly check-in

Between annual reviews, a one-page check-in each quarter — ten minutes, tops. It references the goals we set at your annual review, uses the same traffic-light status as the weekly updates, and always ends with the question I most want answered: *what do you need from me?* We discuss it at the next 1:1.

* **[Quarterly Check-In (fillable PDF)](/assets/pdf/quarterly-checkin.pdf)**

You'll get a Slack ping when each quarter's check-in opens (first week of January, April, July, October). Upload the completed PDF to the *Quarterly Check-Ins* Drive folder as `lastname_2026Q3.pdf` within two weeks.

Neither form is a performance trap. "At risk" or "blocked" is information, not failure — flagging it early is exactly the behavior the lab rewards.
