How we think about research and run projects in the lab: our purpose, expectations, structure, and tools. If you're new, read in this order — it takes about twenty-five minutes and answers most questions before they come up.

1. **[Lab Overview: Read This First](/lab/overview/)** — what we study, how we approach research, and the principles that apply to everyone.
2. **[Lab Expectations: Your Role as a DRI](/lab/expectations/)** — how project ownership develops and what good ownership looks like.
3. **[Starting a Project](/lab/starting-a-project/)** — initialization checklist, the Project Landing Page, and the phases of research.
4. **[Communication Rules](/lab/communication/)** — weekly updates, Slack vs. email, 1:1s, and meeting notes.
5. **[Lab Infrastructure](/lab/infrastructure/)** — the template repository, working documents, Overleaf, and the rest of the toolchain.
6. **[Evaluations & Check-Ins](/lab/evaluations/)** — the annual review, quarterly check-ins, and downloadable forms.

These pages borrow deliberately from Ethan Perez, Jacob Steinhardt, and Ben Kuhn — sources are linked inline where the ideas appear.
